# Tekryno — visual warning review, 26 November 2026

Source: the released `fonts/ttf/Tekryno-Regular.ttf` (SHA-256 `2d642f7844fbc8398caa729801782e2f6c2b9288c47cb3e3b1db8fcec2bffd37`).

A representative, non-invasive visual specimen was rendered with Pillow/FreeType using large 120 px, headline 45 px and smaller 28 px text sizes. Sample content included:
- `2 5 § * ® & Q 8` (contour count, short-segment and near-alignment warnings)
- `Ľ ľ Ď ď Ť ť` (caron placement and letter composition)
- `AV AW VA TA TO PA` (display spacing and kerning)
- `Tekryno 2026!` (intentional uppercase-style lowercase)
- `!?.,:;()[]{}%$&*@`, `0123456789`, selected accented capitals, and a short music headline

## Visual findings

1. **Unusual contour counts:** `two`, `five`, and `section` looked consistent with the sharp, modular aesthetic in the proof. No clear broken path was visible. Do not redesign automatically.
2. **Near-alignment:** `asterisk` and `registered` rendered with coherent outlines at the tested sizes. The source points called out by Fontspector are within 1–2 units of major metrics; retain for now, revisit only if rasterizer-specific artifacts are demonstrated.
3. **Short segments:** The tested numerals, `Q`-style round-square contours and ampersand did not display conspicuous stray fragments at the tested sizes. This is not yet a full high-DPI/low-DPI, all-size test.
4. **Accented/carons:** Representative caron letters and extended capitals rendered without obvious mark collision. **Design decision still pending:** `Ľ/ľ` has a compact apostrophe-like caron at the upper-right, which the alt-caron check warns may be insufficiently distinctive from quote punctuation.
5. **Punctuation and metrics:** Examples were readable as **display typography**, even at 28 px. This is not intended as body-text readability certification.
6. **Auxiliary language/metadata subsets:** Fontspector warns about several *auxiliary* orthography codepoints and subset metadata. These are separate from the independently confirmed GF Latin Core minimum (319/319 encoded). Downstream Google Fonts subsetting and broader language claims need review.
7. **Vendor ID:** `SGPR` is not registered in Fontspector's cached Microsoft registry. Registration is optional; this is a warning rather than evidence of glyph failure.

## Remaining decisions

- [ ] Check whether to keep or improve the `Ľ/ľ` apostrophe-like caron; changes would require original designer sign-off and release versioning.
- [ ] Check mark rendering and punctuation in Illustrator, browsers and different rasterizers at 12–20 px and headline sizes.
- [ ] Review non-core auxiliary-language claims and subset metadata with the GF onboarding engineer if requested.
- [ ] Inspect warning examples at 1x / 2x screen density and printed output.

**Outcome:** No source/binary design changes made. Technical Fontspector report remains 109 PASS, 0 FAIL, 10 WARN. This is a visual *triage* of the warnings, not a claim of completed manual Google Fonts design evaluation.

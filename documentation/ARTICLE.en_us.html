Tekryno is an angular, geometric display typeface designed by Shrawan Gaikwad. The typeface draws inspiration from the energy of electronic music, modular shapes, and futuristic design. Its distinctive uppercase-inspired letters are intended to create a powerful visual presence in posters, music artwork, branding, and headlines. Lowercase input intentionally uses uppercase-style designs.

The family is available in a Regular style and includes a broad Latin character set with accented letters, numerals, and punctuation. The typeface is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

To explore the editable font sources and contribute to the project, visit github.com/sgproductionindia/tekryno.
